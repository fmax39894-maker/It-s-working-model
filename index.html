About the Heart

The human heart is a muscular organ that continuously pumps blood throughout the body. Our working model demonstrates this pumping action in a simple and practical way.

♥
Realistic Action Pumping demonstration of the heart's contraction and relaxation.
●
Blood Flow Shows how blood moves through different parts of circulation.
⚙
Mechanism A simple model helps explain how pumping takes place.
▣
Educational Designed as a practical science exhibition demonstration.

Why Heart Awareness Is Important

Understanding heart health helps people recognize important risk factors and the value of prevention and early care.

Hypertension: High blood pressure can place extra strain on the heart and blood vessels.
Heart disease: Awareness helps people understand important cardiovascular risk factors.
Early detection: Recognizing warning signs can encourage timely medical attention.
Healthy habits: Regular activity and balanced nutrition support overall heart health.
Smoking: Avoiding tobacco helps reduce cardiovascular health risks.
Diabetes: Managing blood sugar is important for long-term cardiovascular health.
Cholesterol: Healthy cholesterol levels can help protect blood vessels.
Regular check-ups: Health checks can identify certain risk factors before complications develop.
Stress and sleep: Good sleep and healthy stress management support overall wellbeing.
Prevention saves lives: Heart-health awareness can encourage safer choices and earlier care.
