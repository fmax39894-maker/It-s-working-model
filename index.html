Why Heart Awareness Is Important

Understanding heart health helps people recognize important risk factors and the value of prevention and early care.

Hypertension can increase the risk of heart disease.
Heart disease is a major cause of death worldwide.
Early awareness can help identify risk factors.
Regular physical activity supports heart health.
A balanced diet can support healthy circulation.
Smoking can significantly harm cardiovascular health.
Diabetes can increase cardiovascular risk.
Knowing warning signs can encourage timely medical help.
Regular health checks can help monitor blood pressure.
Heart health education encourages healthier choices.

Learn the main functions of the heart and how blood moves through the chambers.

1
⏻

Receives Blood

The atria receive blood returning to the heart.

2
♥

Pumps Blood

3
◆

Blood Circulation

↻
